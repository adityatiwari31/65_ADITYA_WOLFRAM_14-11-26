# Wolfram Demonstrations - Mathematics for AIML

## Overview

This repository contains a collection of statistical demonstrations created using Wolfram. The project explores important probability distributions and statistical concepts through interactive visualizations, graphs, and explanations.

The demonstrations help understand how different probability distributions behave and how their properties change with different parameters.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Binomial Distribution via Coin Flips
- Simulates repeated coin flips and records the number of heads.
- Explores the effect of changing the probability of getting heads.
- Allows different numbers of coin flips per trial (5, 10, and 20).
- Displays the bar chart, probability mass function (PMF), and binomial distribution.
- Demonstrates how the distribution approaches a normal-like shape under suitable conditions.

### 2. Standard Normal Distribution Areas
- Explores areas under the standard normal curve.
- Demonstrates cumulative probabilities using adjustable Z-values.
- Explains left-tail, right-tail, and interval probabilities.
- Illustrates the 68–95–99.7 empirical rule.

### 3. Chi-Squared Distribution and Central Limit Theorem
- Explores the chi-squared distribution for different degrees of freedom.
- Compares chi-squared and standard normal distributions.
- Displays Probability Density Functions (PDF) and Cumulative Distribution Functions (CDF).
- Demonstrates how the standardized chi-squared distribution approaches the standard normal distribution as the degrees of freedom increase.

### 4. Student's t-Distribution
- Explores the Student's t-distribution and its relationship with the standard normal distribution.
- Demonstrates the effect of changing degrees of freedom.
- Compares t-density, Z-density, and cumulative distribution functions.
- Explains the application of the t-distribution in estimating parameters of an unknown normal population.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Wolfram:** Used for statistical demonstrations and visualizations.
- **Statistical Graphs:** Used to understand probability distributions and their behavior.

## Learning Outcomes

Through these demonstrations, the project helps in understanding:

- Probability distributions and their characteristics.
- Binomial probability and repeated independent trials.
- Standard normal distribution and Z-scores.
- Chi-squared distribution and the Central Limit Theorem.
- Student's t-distribution and degrees of freedom.
- Graphical interpretation of statistical concepts.
